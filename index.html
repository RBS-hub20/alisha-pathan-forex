About

My Journey — From Beginner to Funded Trader

When I first stepped into Forex, I honestly knew nothing.

Charts felt confusing. Strategies sounded complicated. I didn't understand how the market actually moves. I made mistakes, I faced losses, and there were moments I really doubted myself.

But I didn't give up.

I chose to learn. To show up every day. To turn every mistake into a lesson and every challenge into growth. Step by step, my confusion turned into clarity, and my struggles became my strength.

Today, as a Forex and Funded Trader, my mission is to make this journey easier for you.

I know exactly how it feels to start from zero — the overwhelm, the fear, the feeling of being lost. That's why I created this community: to simplify Gold trading, to teach you discipline, confidence, and the right mindset — without the confusion I went through.

Because I've been where you are. And I want to help you grow with the right knowledge and guidance.

I'm Alisha Pathan — Your Forex Girl.

FAQ

Questions, Answered

Is the community really free?+

Yes — there's no separate membership fee. Access comes with an eligible account at one of our partner brokers. Your trading capital stays in your own broker account.

Do I need trading experience?+

Not at all. The video library starts with the basics — setting up MetaTrader, placing trades and managing risk — and the live sessions are beginner-friendly.

How are the Gold trade ideas shared?+

Approximately 5–7 XAU/USD ideas are posted each trading day in our private Telegram group, with entry, stop-loss and take-profit levels. Always apply your own risk management.

When are the live Zoom sessions?+

Sessions run regularly each week, and the schedule is shared inside the community. Replays and key lessons are added to the library.

Is profit guaranteed?+

No. Trading carries real risk and losses are part of it. We focus on education, discipline and risk management — never guaranteed returns.
